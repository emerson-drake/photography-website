Iowa City Couple Photographer

Your love, exactly as it feels.

Not just photos. Your love story.

Every relationship has its own rhythm. Maybe yours is filled with spontaneous adventures, quiet coffee dates, or laughing until your stomach hurts. Whatever your story looks like, it deserves to be documented authentically.

As an Iowa City couple photographer, Emerson Drake creates romantic, candid photographs that celebrate the connection between two people. Forget stiff poses and forced smiles. Your session is about being together, having fun, and making memories along the way.

Couples Photography in Iowa City

From the historic streets of downtown Iowa City to the peaceful trails at Terry Trueblood Recreation Area, there are countless beautiful backdrops for your couples session.

We love photographing couples at sunset, when the soft golden light brings warmth and a cinematic feeling to every image. Whether you're celebrating an anniversary, an engagement, or simply being in love, your session will reflect your relationship.

The Couples Experience

What's Included

Every couples session includes a planning consultation, a personalized location recommendation, up to 60 minutes of photography, and 35 professionally edited digital images delivered in a private online gallery.

Session investment: $275

What to Expect

We'll walk, talk, laugh, and create moments that feel completely natural. I'll guide you with simple prompts while leaving room for genuine interactions. You don't need modeling experience, just each other.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should we wear?

Choose outfits that feel comfortable and complement each other without matching exactly. Neutral colors, soft textures, and subtle patterns photograph beautifully.

Can we bring our dog?

Absolutely! Pets are part of the story too. We can choose a pet-friendly outdoor location and plan time for a few photographs together.

When should we schedule our session?

Spring and fall are especially beautiful in Iowa City. For soft, romantic lighting, sessions typically begin about an hour before sunset.
