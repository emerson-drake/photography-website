Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to pose?

Yes. Standard sessions allow one outfit change, while extended sessions provide more flexibility for creative styling.

Popular options include downtown Iowa City, outdoor parks, and urban architectural spaces. Locations are selected based on your vision and the season.
