1. Page and Element Being Tested

For my A/B test, I will test the main call-to-action (CTA) button on the homepage of my photography website, by.emersondrake. My website focuses on cinematic photography for couples, portraits, and graduation sessions in Iowa City.

Version A (Control): "EXPLORE THE EXPERIENCE" (current homepage)

Version B (Treatment): "BOOK YOUR SESSION"

The only difference between the versions will be the CTA button text. The layout, images, colors, and remaining website content will stay the same.

2. Hypothesis

I predict that Version B will lead to a higher percentage of visitors saying they would contact the photographer about booking a session. My reasoning is that "BOOK YOUR SESSION" gives visitors a clearer and more direct next step, while "EXPLORE THE EXPERIENCE" encourages browsing. Since my goal is to attract potential photography clients, I want to test whether more action-oriented wording increases interest in booking.

3. AI Visitors and Random Assignment

I plan to use OpenAI's GPT-6 model through ChatGPT for all 100 AI visitor trials, keeping the model and available settings consistent throughout the experiment. Each trial will take place in a new conversation so previous responses do not influence the results.

Each AI visitor will represent a college student in Iowa City interested in booking a portrait or graduation photography session.

Exact prompt:

"You are a college student in Iowa City looking for a photographer for an upcoming portrait or graduation session. Review the following photography website homepage as a potential customer. Based only on the information provided, would you contact this photographer about booking a session? Answer yes or no, followed by one sentence explaining your reasoning."

I will provide the full homepage text for Version A or Version B in the same format each time. Before collecting responses, I will create a randomized sequence containing 50 assignments to Version A and 50 to Version B, and record each assignment.

4. Primary Outcome

My primary outcome will be whether the AI visitor says they would contact the photographer about booking a session.

Yes = 1

No = 0

I will calculate the percentage of "yes" responses for each version.

5. Sample Size and Timeline

I will conduct 100 total trials, with 50 assigned to Version A and 50 assigned to Version B. I plan to publish Version B and begin testing by November 17, 2026, and finish collecting data by November 23, 2026. I will complete all 100 trials regardless of the results.

6. Comparison and Decision

I will compare the percentage of "yes" responses between Versions A and B and use a two-proportion statistical test to evaluate whether the difference could be due to chance. My hypothesis predicts a higher "yes" rate for Version B. I will consider both the size of the difference and statistical uncertainty before deciding whether to adopt the new CTA.

I will record every trial, including results that do not support my hypothesis. I will also acknowledge that AI visitors are simulated customers, so their responses may not accurately reflect how real people would behave.
