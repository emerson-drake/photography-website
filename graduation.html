Best Places for Graduation Photos in Iowa City

Your graduation photos should reflect the places that made your college experience unforgettable. As an Iowa City graduation photographer, I love incorporating meaningful campus locations into each session to create images that feel personal, timeless, and uniquely yours.

Old Capitol & The Pentacrest

Nothing says University of Iowa graduation quite like the Old Capitol. Its beautiful architecture and iconic steps create the perfect backdrop for classic cap-and-gown portraits. The surrounding Pentacrest lawn also offers plenty of space for natural, candid photographs with friends.

Downtown Iowa City

For seniors who want something a little more creative, downtown Iowa City offers charming storefronts, textured brick walls, and lively streets. It's one of my favorite locations for editorial-inspired graduation portraits that combine personal style with the energy of your college town.

The Iowa River

If you're dreaming of soft, romantic graduation photos, the Iowa River provides a peaceful setting with beautiful natural light. Golden hour along the river creates warm, cinematic images that celebrate both your accomplishments and the beginning of your next chapter.

Whether you prefer iconic campus landmarks or something more personal, we'll choose locations that make your graduation session feel like you.

Graduation Photography FAQs

When should I book graduation photos?

For spring graduation, March through early May is popular. Booking four to eight weeks ahead provides more flexibility with dates and locations.

What if it rains?

We can adjust the session date or choose a covered location when possible. Iowa weather can be unpredictable, so flexibility is always part of the plan.

Can my friends join my session?

Yes! Friends are welcome for a few shared photographs, and separate group packages can be arranged for larger celebrations.

When will I receive my photos?

Final edited photographs are delivered through a private online gallery within two weeks of your session.
